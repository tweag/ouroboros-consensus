### Non-Breaking

- Add `applyBlockLedgerResultWithPerasContext` to `ApplyBlock`: the same as
  `applyBlockLedgerResultWithValidation`, with what the ledger needs to validate
  the Peras certificate of the block (`PerasApplyContext`, from the new module
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Peras.ApplyContext`: the nonces and rounds of the epoch of
  the block and of the one before it). Its default ignores the context.
- Add `getPerasPrevNonceFromTickedHeaderState` to
  `StateSupportsPerasEpochContext`, with a default for blocks without Peras
  certificates, and `perasApplyContext`, which builds the context from a ticked
  resolver and header state.
- `ExtLedgerState` passes the context to the ledger when applying a block, and
  the Shelley ledger passes it on to `cardano-ledger`, which validates the Peras
  certificate of a Dijkstra block body.

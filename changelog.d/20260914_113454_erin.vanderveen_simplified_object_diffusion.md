### Breaking

- Add `opwClassifyObjectId` to `ObjectPoolWriter` so clients can distinguish
  requestable, too-new and too-old object IDs, and expose local request blockage
  through `ObjectDiffusionInboundState` and `ObjectDiffusionInboundStateView`.

### Non-Breaking

- Make Peras certificate diffusion request only the validatable prefix of the
  peer's outstanding FIFO. Pause locally at the validation horizon and resume
  when the Peras epoch-context resolver advances, without treating the peer as
  idling or affecting vote diffusion.

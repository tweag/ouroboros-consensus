### Breaking

- The Peras round number, seat index, block reference and boosted block types
  now come from `cardano-crypto-peras` and are re-exported by
  `Ouroboros.Consensus.Peras.Types`; `Bytes32RealPoint` is replaced by
  `PerasBlockRef` and `Ouroboros.Consensus.Util.Bitmap` is removed in favour of
  `Data.Bitmap` from `cardano-strict-containers`.
- `V1.PerasCert` is a newtype over `Cardano.Crypto.Peras.Cert.PerasCert`;
  `V1.PerasCertVoters` and the compact voters representation are removed (use
  `perasCertVoterSeats`/`perasCertVotersFromSeats` from `cardano-crypto-peras`).
  The voters bitmap is now MSB-first within a byte (seat i is bit 7 - (i mod 8)
  of byte (i div 8)); the rest of the wire format is unchanged.
- `PerasConversionError` gains a `PerasCertVotersError` constructor.

### Non-Breaking

- Add `mkSignature`/`rawSignature` conversions between raw BLS signatures and
  role-tagged ones to `Ouroboros.Consensus.Committee.Crypto.BLS`.

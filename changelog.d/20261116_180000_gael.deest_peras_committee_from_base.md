### Breaking

- Remove `Ouroboros.Consensus.Committee.*` and `Ouroboros.Consensus.Peras.Crypto.BLS`:
  the voting committee code and `PerasBLSCrypto` moved, unchanged, to
  `cardano-crypto-peras` (`Cardano.Crypto.Peras.Committee.*` and
  `Cardano.Crypto.Peras.Crypto.BLS`), where the ledger can use them.
- The committee code now takes the mirrors of the ledger types from
  `Cardano.Crypto.Peras.Committee.Base` (`Nonce`, `KeyHash`, `BlsKey`, `NonZero`).
  Add `Ouroboros.Consensus.Peras.LedgerConversions` to convert the ledger's to them.
- Move the `NoThunks` instance for `Array` from `Ouroboros.Consensus.Util.Orphans`
  to `cardano-crypto-peras`.
